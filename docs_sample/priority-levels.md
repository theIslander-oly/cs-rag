## Priority Levels

TicketFlow uses four priority levels, each with its own response-time SLA.

### P1 — Critical

A P1 outage affects all users or a revenue-critical system. Response SLA:
15 minutes, 24/7. P1 tickets page the on-call agent immediately regardless
of business hours.

### P2 — High

A P2 issue affects a single team or a non-critical system with no workaround.
Response SLA: 2 business hours.

### P3 — Normal

The default priority for most requests: individual access issues, minor bugs,
hardware requests. Response SLA: 1 business day.

### P4 — Low

Cosmetic issues, feature requests, and anything with an easy workaround.
Response SLA: 5 business days.

Priority is set automatically from the ticket's category on creation, but any
agent can override it manually. Overriding priority does not require manager
approval for P2–P4; lowering or raising to/from P1 requires a team lead to
confirm the change.
